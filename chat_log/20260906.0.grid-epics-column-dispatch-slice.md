## Key Insights
- `Signal(observable)` already wraps in `shareReplay({refCount:true})` (`1_SignalCreator.ts:80`); epic lifetime belongs to a subscription, never a counter.
- Sync Subject feedback reorders delivery for later observers; `observeOn(queueScheduler)` must sit before the multicast (`share()`), not per subscriber.
- TanStack v9 `createSortedRowModel` sorts subRows recursively; `manualSorting` must be false for trees.
- Playwright clicks fail when a deep-indent twisty sits past its column; indent as a shrinkable flex spacer keeps the twisty inside.
- `git add -A` under a temp `GIT_INDEX_FILE` yields a full patch of uncommitted+untracked work without touching the real index; a review lane applies it in its worktree.
